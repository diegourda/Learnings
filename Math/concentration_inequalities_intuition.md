# Concentration Inequalities: Intuition First

## Core question
How fast does empirical behavior converge to expectation?

## Key results
- Hoeffding: bounded RVs
- Bernstein: variance-aware
- Chernoff: exponential tails

## Intuition
All concentration is about **tail decay vs independence**.

## Why this matters
- Overfitting detection
- Risk bounds
- Strategy robustness
